We checked 162 Japanese SaaS sites — the front door is open, the room is unreadable

Almost none of them block AI crawlers. Far more of them are unreadable once the crawler is inside.

In this sample of 162 leading Japanese SaaS domains, 2.5% block AI crawlers — but 41.4% do not put an address or phone number anywhere a machine can read, and 75.9% publish no llms.txt. The door is open; what is behind it is hard to read.

What was measured

Each domain was fetched with the same public API behind the free check on this site — anyone can reproduce these numbers with the same tool. For each domain we retrieved the public pages plus robots.txt, llms.txt and sitemap.xml, and scored whether AI crawlers can read it and whether a machine can tell what the business is. No logged-in areas were touched.

What it reads as

  • The entrance is open. Blocking AI crawlers is rare here. "Your site is configured to keep AI out" is not, for these companies, the common problem.
  • The problem is further in. Sites that can be read still leave the machine unable to find an address, or to be told what the business is.
  • What separates the top from the bottom is unglamorous — structured data and contact details, not advanced tactics.

FAQ

How many companies is this?

162 leading Japanese SaaS domains, drawn from the 200 services verified for ARI Award 2026 Summer and de-duplicated by domain. 19 domains could not be fetched and are excluded.

Do you publish per-company scores?

No. This is a lightweight readability check, not the ARI rating; attaching names would blur the two. Only the distribution is published.

Does a low score mean AI will not recommend that company?

No. The check covers whether AI can read the site and tell what the business is. Whether a company gets recommended depends on competitors, third-party mentions and other factors. Nobody can guarantee ranking.

Method and limits

Measured 2026-09-03 with the same public API as the free check on this site. Target set: 181 Japanese SaaS domains resolved from the 200 services verified for ARI Award 2026 Summer, de-duplicated by domain; 162 were fetched successfully and 19 are excluded (DNS failures and redirect loops, still failing after retrying with a www. prefix). One point in time; sites change. This measures machine readability. It is not a quality judgement of any company and not the ARI rating. Per-company scores are withheld to avoid confusion with the rating.

What we do not promise: we cannot guarantee visibility or ranking inside any AI product, and we do not treat a single AI answer as market share. Principles of Independence
