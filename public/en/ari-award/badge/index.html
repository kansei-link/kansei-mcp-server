ARI AWARD 2026 SUMMER

Using Your Certification Badge

Services certified in ARI Award 2026 Summer may use the badges below on their websites, press releases, and sales materials.

Certification Badges

Download the badge that matches your certification grade (free). To protect the brand, we ask you to register your company, service name and email address before downloading. Badges are transparent PNGs (500×500) that work on both light and dark backgrounds.

AAA
Score 90+
Highest rating — official MCP + OAuth 2.0 + complete documentation + package distribution
AA
Score 80–89
High rating — official MCP support + a solid API foundation
A
Score 70–79
Certified — provides the foundation agents need

Badge Usage Guidelines

Please use the certification badge in accordance with the following guidelines.

OK

  • ✓Display on your service's website and landing pages
  • ✓State "ARI Award 2026 Summer certified" in press releases
  • ✓Include in sales materials and proposals
  • ✓Announce your certification on social media

Not permitted

  • ✕Altering the badge's colors, shape, or text
  • ✕Using the badge for a service that was not certified
  • ✕Misrepresenting rating results (including score figures)
  • ✕Implying an official endorsement by KanseiLink beyond the rating

Press Release Template

Feel free to use the template below when announcing your certification.

[Service name] receives [AAA/AA/A] certification in KanseiLink's "ARI Award 2026 Summer" [Service name], provided by [Company name], has been certified [AAA/AA/A] in "ARI Award 2026 Summer" (organized by KanseiLink), which evaluates how ready SaaS services are for the age of AI agents. ARI Award 2026 Summer rates 200 major SaaS services in the Japanese market by verifying MCP (Model Context Protocol) support, API availability, authentication methods, and developer documentation against primary sources, with the full scoring formula publicly disclosed.
