Usage guidelines

Permitted

  • Display the badge for the certified service and grade.
  • Use it on your corporate website, press release and sales materials.
  • Resize it proportionally while preserving legibility.

Not permitted

  • Change the grade, wording, colors or proportions.
  • Use the badge for a service that was not certified.
  • Imply an endorsement beyond the published ARI rating.
