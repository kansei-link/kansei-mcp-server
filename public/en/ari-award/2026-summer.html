Methodology (Fully Disclosed)

Ratings are rules-based evaluations under the AXR (Agent Experience Rating) framework. No subjective LLM judgement is used. The same checklist is applied to every service, based solely on each vendor's primary sources (developer documentation, press releases, official repositories). Data collected by KanseiLink itself (integration recipe counts, measured connections, usage statistics) is never used in Award scoring — the depth of our own database cannot influence any service's grade. Grade bands: AAA (90+) / AA (80+) / A (70+) / BBB (60+) / BB (50+) / B (40+) / C (30+) / D (below 30).

What we say openly

  • We do not publish per-service connection success rates. Current measurements come mostly from our own automated probes and have not yet reached the scale of collective intelligence from many independent agents. We will publish the full version once the measurement infrastructure matures.
  • This Index rates "technical readiness to be chosen by AI" (AI Access). It is not an overall evaluation of each service's features, quality, or security.
  • Every published fact has been verified, service by service, against vendor primary sources (developer documentation, press releases, official help pages, public openapi.json) as of 2026-07-16. If you find an error, contact us — we will correct it immediately and record the correction on this page.
  • For rating independence, see our Independence Policy. Scores are not for sale.
