2026 Summer certification

ARI evaluates whether an AI agent can discover, understand, authenticate with and safely use a service. Ratings are based on public evidence and measured integration signals—not paid placement.

AAA

Best-in-class readiness. Clear access, documentation, authentication and trust signals.

AA

Highly agent-ready, with only limited friction in onboarding or execution.

A

Meets the certification threshold and provides a credible path for agent use.

How the ratings are produced

01

Primary-source review

Vendor documentation, API references, authentication guides and official MCP availability are checked directly.

02

Rules-based scoring

The published formula is applied consistently. No subjective LLM judgement determines the grade.

03

Vendor verification

Certified vendors can submit corrections and supporting evidence for review.
