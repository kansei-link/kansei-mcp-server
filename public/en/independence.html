Independence policy

Our ratings are designed to remain useful to buyers, providers, investors and AI agents without being shaped by paid placement.

Core principles

Evidence first

Ratings rely on published vendor evidence, measurable integration signals and disclosed scoring rules.

No pay-to-rate

Payment, advertising or commercial relationships do not determine whether a service is included or what grade it receives.

Corrections are welcome

Vendors may submit primary-source evidence. Corrections are reviewed under the same rules applied to every service.

Methodology disclosed

Rating criteria and material methodology changes are published so results can be scrutinized.

Certification badges

Badges communicate the published grade for a specific service and award period. Their use does not imply a broader commercial endorsement.
